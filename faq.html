Simply visit our Track Package page, enter your unique tracking ID (e.g. GX-A1B2C3D4E5), and click "Track". You will instantly see your package's current status, high-precision map pin, and full milestone history.

GoExpressly tracking numbers always begin with the prefix GX- followed by 10 alphanumeric characters (for example, GX-VSVMCTRXU8). You can find this code in your booking confirmation email or receipt.

"Customs Clearance" indicates your parcel has arrived at an international border facility (such as a USPS International Mail Facility) and is undergoing standard documentation inspection. Our map displays the exact facility name and coordinates so you know precisely where your package is held.

Our maps operate at Zoom Level 16 (building-level precision) using Esri World Imagery. Administrators manually adjust pins directly onto physical buildings, so you see exact facility roofs rather than generic road intersections.
